Your company knowledge lives in many places at once: PDFs, specs, spreadsheets, wikis, ERP exports. Classic RAG retrieves the top-K most similar fragments and hopes the answer is in there. That works for summaries; it fails when answers must be exact, complete, and follow business rules.

Vedana takes a structure-first approach. You describe your domain as a data model: anchors (entities), attributes (facts), and links (relations). Vedana loads your data into a typed knowledge graph that follows this model, and AI agents explore it step by step: issuing structured graph queries, running vector search, verifying sources, and assembling answers from real data. The LLM interprets the data, and the data stays the source of truth.

What you get:
- Exact values: real prices, dates, and statuses fetched straight from the graph
- Complete results: queries return every matching record, with nothing silently dropped
- Multi-hop reasoning: product to category to regulation to document
- Traceability: every answer maps back to nodes, edges, and source chunks
- Determinism: the same question triggers the same operations

An evaluation harness with golden datasets keeps quality measurable. Connect via HTTP API, web widget, or Telegram; works with any LLM provider. Built for product catalogs, legal and compliance, internal knowledge bases, and B2B support, anywhere answers must follow constraints. Open-core, managed cloud, or on-premise. A structured pilot ships in 4 weeks.
