# Vedana Use Cases

Vedana fits wherever answers must be exact, complete, and rule-bound.

## E-commerce & product catalogs

Current prices and stock, attribute filtering ("all frost-free fridges under 100k"), product comparison, availability across branches, mapping products to regulatory requirements. Top-K retrieval misses items; graph queries return the full set.

## Legal & compliance

Which regulations apply to a product, contract, or category; which documents govern which area; deadlines, approval statuses, blockers. Multi-hop reasoning across documents and regulators, with every conclusion traceable to source.

## Internal knowledge bases

Policies, regulations, and instructions with citations; "who is responsible for X"; process explanations grounded in source documents; onboarding support backed by the actual org structure.

## B2B & dealer support

Stable answers to recurring dealer questions: export specs, price lists, partner terms, compatibility checks, configurators. Same question, same answer, every time, in every conversation.

## HR & org structure

Who works on which project, who owns a process or system, reporting lines and escalation chains, schedules and on-call rotations.

## Finance & reporting

Policies, limits, and procedures; structured queries about deal statuses and contracts; navigating reports without copy-pasting them into a prompt.

---

## Where Vedana is the wrong tool

Free-form summarization, creative writing, code/API doc search, and real-time analytics over huge volumes: specialised tools do these better. Vedana deliberately constrains the LLM; if your task benefits from an unconstrained one, use classic RAG.
